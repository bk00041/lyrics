Intro
Me pareció ver una linda gatita, doblete
I thought I saw a cute kitten, Doblete
Slang & Reference: "Gatita" (literally "kitty") is Urban Spanish slang for an attractive girl. "Doblete" (W) is Wisin's signature nickname. The opening line playfulness references the classic Looney Tunes Sylvester catchphrase.
Verse 1
Me desperté, en la sala de una casa ajena
I woke up in the living room of someone else's house
Y tropecé, con dos tacones y cuatro botellas
And I stumbled over two high heels and four bottles
Voy recordando, la cosa sí que estuvo buena
I'm remembering now, things really were good
Y me encontré, en el sofá con un par de morenas
And I found myself on the couch with a couple of brunettes
Cultural Note: "Morena" in Spanish can refer to women with dark hair, tan skin, or brown/black complexions depending on regional context.
Pre-Chorus
Pa' qué seguir trabajando, yo sigo celebrando
Why keep working? I'll keep celebrating
Grammar Note: "Pa'" is a standard spoken contraction of "Para" in casual Spanish.
Así la vida se vive mejor
Life is lived better this way
Anoche fue una locura, mañana es otra aventura
Last night was crazy, tomorrow is another adventure
Quizás pasado me olvide de hoy
Maybe the day after tomorrow I'll forget about today
Vocabulary: "Pasado" here is short for "pasado mañana" (the day after tomorrow).
Chorus
Yo no necesito vacaciones, ni dolores de cabeza
I don't need vacations, nor headaches
Solo me basta mis amigos y un traguito de cerveza
All I need is my friends and a sip of beer
Diminutive Breakdown: "Traguito" is the affectionate diminutive of "trago" (drink/sip).
Solo quiero que me avisen si esta noche hay una fiesta
I just want them to let me know if there's a party tonight
Hook
Lelolelola y sudando
Lelolelola and sweating
Lelolelola y guayando
Lelolelola and dancing close
Reggaeton Slang: "Guayando" comes from "guayar" (literally to grate/rub against). In Caribbean urban music, it means grinding or dancing very close body-to-body. "Lelolelo" borrows from traditional Puerto Rican folk ad-libs (jíbaro music).
Lelolelola y perreando
Lelolelola and perreando
Dance Culture: "Perreando" refers to "perreo," the signature low-squatting grinding dance style intrinsic to classic Reggaeton.
Lelolelola y vacilando
Lelolelola and having fun
Slang Breakdown: "Vacilando" comes from "vacilar," meaning to party, fool around, joke, or enjoy life without taking things seriously.
