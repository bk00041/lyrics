Verse 1 (Karol G)
Yo no ando con nadie, pero me tienen velá
I'm not with anyone, but they're keeping a close eye on me
Slang Breakdown: "Me tienen velá" comes from Colombian/Caribbean slang meaning to be constantly watched, stalked, or closely monitored by suitors or paparazzi.
Ya 'toy elevada, me siento Gatúbela

Title Reference: "Gatúbela" is the official Spanish name for DC Comics' Catwoman. Karol G uses this persona to represent female empowerment, sexual liberation, and predatory dominance.

Phrase Meaning: "No estar pintado/a" literally means "not painted." Idiomatically, it means "I'm not a fake or a decoration—I'm the real deal."

Genre Context: "Perreo" refers to old-school, raw, danceable reggaeton rhythm and party culture. Maldy (formerly of Plan B) is considered a legend of early 2000s perreo.

Puerto Rican Term: "Bellaqueo" refers explicitly to provocative grinding, flirting, or intense physical passion on the dance floor.
Te quiero dar mazacote pa' que esas nalgas reboten
