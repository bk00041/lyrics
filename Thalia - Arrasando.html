Chorus Excerpt
Arrasando con la vida
Sweeping through life / Crushing it in life
Vocabulary: Arrasar means to sweep past obstacles, triumph effortlessly, or succeed on a massive scale.
Cosechando la alegría
Harvesting joy
No hay obstáculo que me impida
There is no obstacle that keeps me
Disfrutar de un nuevo día
From enjoying a brand new day
Key Expressions & Idioms
Vas sintiendo cuando subes que te dan zancadillas
You feel as you rise that they try to trip you up
Idiom Breakdown: Dar zancadillas translates literally to "tripping someone," used idiomatically to describe intentional sabotage or undermining someone's progress.
Nunca faltan los gorrones para ir de reventones
There’s never a shortage of freeloaders to go partying
Mexican Slang: Gorrones refers to moochers or free-riders, while reventones are wild, loud parties.
Que si engordo, que si enflaco, que si no tengo costillas
Whether I get fat, whether I get skinny, whether I have no ribs
Cultural Note: A direct, tongue-in-cheek response to the famous Latin media urban legend claiming Thalía surgically removed her ribs to achieve an unusually small waistline.
Einstein, DaVinci, Neruda y la Malinche
Einstein, Da Vinci, Neruda, and La Malinche
Historical Reference: La Malinche was an Indigenous Nahua woman who acted as an interpreter, adviser, and intermediary during the Spanish conquest of Mexico.
